2. How we use data

We use account information to authenticate you, manage access and provide support. We process submitted content to store, display and manage it, and to carry out publishing instructions when the relevant channel is configured and authorized. We use technical data to operate, secure and troubleshoot the service. We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising.

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, the existing policy's intended legal bases are contract for providing the service, legitimate interests for proportionate security and reliability work, legal obligations where applicable, and consent where requested. Their application to this deployment remains subject to owner review.

3. Google sign-in

Google sign-in requests access to your basic profile and email address. The current sign-in handler uses your Google account identifier and email address to identify or create your PostDelegate account. Google authorization credentials are exchanged during sign-in so the server can obtain that information; PostDelegate then issues its own application session.

These sign-in permissions do not authorize access to your Gmail messages, Drive files, Calendar events or YouTube channel content, and do not authorize posting to a social channel. Any publishing connection requires its own permissions and configuration.

We use Google information for sign-in, account access, support and security of those features. We do not use it for advertising, sale or training generalized AI models. Our handling of Google data must follow the Google API Services User Data Policy, including applicable Limited Use requirements. Google's own processing is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

You can revoke PostDelegate's Google authorization in your Google Account's third-party connections settings. Revoking authorization is separate from requesting deletion of records already held by PostDelegate.

4. Hosting and other recipients

  • OVHcloud: the application server and its databases run on the existing OVH VPS.
  • Cloudflare: DNS, proxy/CDN and security services handle request metadata; R2 stores uploaded media.
  • Google: provides the optional Google sign-in flow described above.
  • Your authorized recipients: workspace members according to access permissions, and any separately configured social platform to which you instruct the service to publish.

No AI generation provider, outbound transactional-email provider or payment/billing provider is active in this deployment.

We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect rights and safety. Any ownership transfer remains subject to this policy and applicable law.

5. Uploaded media is publicly retrievable

The configured media host, media.postdelegate.com, serves uploaded objects through public HTTPS URLs without application sign-in. Anyone who obtains a media URL can retrieve that object. Upload only material that may be publicly accessible. Removing an application record is not, by itself, a verified guarantee that the stored object or every cached copy has been removed.

6. Cookies and browser storage

The application uses cookies and browser storage for sign-in, organization selection, language and display preferences, timezone settings and authentication flows. Cloudflare may use security-related mechanisms when handling requests.

Owner/deployment confirmation required before publication: whether optional tracking integrations are enabled, and which additional cookies or recipients they introduce.

7. Retention and deletion

Data may be held in the live application, uploaded media, operational logs and recovery backups. Preserved records and backups from the earlier deployment remain separate from the current live Postiz database.

Owner confirmation required before publication: retention periods for account/content records, logs, media and backups, and a verified deletion process across those copies.

To request access, correction, export or deletion, email support@postdelegate.com. We may need to verify your identity and clarify which account or workspace the request concerns. We will explain any applicable legal retention or third-party rights that affect the request and report the request's completion status.

8. Security

The public application and media host use HTTPS. Application databases and supporting services are not exposed through public database ports, and local account passwords are hashed. No system is perfectly secure; report suspected vulnerabilities to support@postdelegate.com.

The operator is Nasida Apps LLC; its legal jurisdiction remains subject to the confirmation above. OVHcloud and Cloudflare may process data in locations associated with the configured server, storage and edge services.

Owner confirmation required before publication: the actual VPS/R2 processing locations and any applicable cross-border transfer arrangements. No specific hosting region or executed transfer mechanism is asserted here.

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or export your data, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, or complain to a data protection authority. Contact the support address above. If your data was submitted through another customer's workspace, identify that customer so the request can be directed appropriately.

10. Children

PostDelegate is not intended for anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.
