Data Deletion Instructions

PostDelegate is operated by Nasida Apps LLC.

You can remove a connected social account, delete your PostDelegate account, or ask us to delete data we hold about you. Each option is described below.

1. Disconnect a social account

  1. Sign in at app.postdelegate.com.
  2. In the channel list, open the menu on the channel you want to remove and choose to disable or delete it.

PostDelegate then stops using that connection. You can also revoke PostDelegate's access on the platform itself at any time. On Facebook and Instagram, go to Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations (or Apps and websites) and remove PostDelegate. On TikTok, LinkedIn, YouTube and other platforms, use the connected apps section of that platform's security or privacy settings.

2. Delete your PostDelegate account

  1. Sign in and open Settings.
  2. Choose Delete account and confirm.

This removes your account from active use. Workspaces that only you belong to are deleted too, along with their connected channels and those channels' stored access tokens. Workspaces shared with other people keep their channels; ask the workspace owner to disconnect them.

3. Ask us to delete your data

Email support@postdelegate.com with the subject “Data deletion request”. Send it from the email address on your account, or tell us which account, workspace or connected social profile the request concerns. We may ask you to verify your identity.

We confirm receipt and complete verified requests within 30 days. We then confirm what was deleted and explain anything we must keep, for example for legal, security or fraud-prevention reasons. Copies in recovery backups expire on our normal backup rotation and are not restored into active use.

4. Content already published

Deleting PostDelegate data does not delete posts that were already published to a social platform. Remove those directly on that platform.

More detail is in our Privacy Policy.
