[LinkedIn personal profiles](/docs/channels/linkedin) and [TikTok](/docs/channels/tiktok) are available now; the pending LinkedIn destination above is company Pages only. X is not available on PostDelegate.

We do not publish dates, because approval timelines belong to each network. When a connection opens it will appear under **Add Channel** in the app and get a guide here.

In the meantime you can plan and schedule for the [networks that work today](/docs/channels), and you can still [tell us what you need](mailto:support@postdelegate.com) so we can prioritise.
