Facebook Pages are available to connect today. PostDelegate publishes to the Facebook Pages you manage. It does not post to personal profiles or to groups.

## What you need

- A Facebook account that manages the Page, with permission to create content on it.
- For a story, at least one photo or video.

## Connect

1. On the calendar page choose **Add Channel**, then **Facebook Page**.
2. Sign in with Facebook and approve the permissions. Facebook asks which Pages to share with PostDelegate. Choose the Pages you want to post to, and the businesses they belong to if Facebook asks.
3. Back in PostDelegate, choose the Page to connect.
4. The channel appears on your calendar.

Each Page is its own channel. To connect another Page, choose **Add Channel** and **Facebook Page** again, then pick the other Page. PostDelegate never sees your Facebook password.

If the Page you want is not in the list, it was not shared with PostDelegate during sign-in, or your Facebook account does not manage it. Add the channel again and choose that Page when Facebook asks.

## What you can post

| Post type | What goes out |
| --- | --- |
| Text | A text post on the Page, up to 63,206 characters. You can attach a link preview or a background. |
| Photos | A post with one or more photos and the post text. |
| Video | A video post with the post text. You can add a video title. |
| Story | Each photo or video becomes its own story on the Page. A story needs at least one photo or video. |

## Post settings

| Setting | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Post Type | **Post** or **Story**. Defaults to **Post**. |
| Embedded URL | Optional, for text posts without media. Facebook shows the link as a preview card. A link typed in the post text alone shows as plain text. |
| Video Title | Optional, for video posts. Not used on stories. |
| Background | Optional, for text-only posts shorter than 130 characters. If Facebook no longer offers the background you picked, the post is published as plain text. |

## Good to know

- If you remove PostDelegate's access to a Page in Facebook, or lose your role on the Page, the channel shows a reconnect prompt and its scheduled posts will not publish. Choose **Reconnect channel** and sign in with Facebook again, sharing the Page. See [Reconnecting a channel](/docs/channels#reconnecting-a-channel).
- Facebook may take a few minutes to process a video before it appears on the Page.
- Instagram and Threads are separate connections and are still waiting on approval. See [Waiting on approval](/docs/channels/coming-soon).
- To remove everything PostDelegate holds, see [Privacy and data deletion](/docs/privacy).
