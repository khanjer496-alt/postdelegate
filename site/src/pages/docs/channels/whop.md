## Connect Whop

Choose **Add Channel**, then **Whop**, and sign in with the Whop account that can access the destination.

## Choose a destination

In the post settings, select a **Company**, then a **Forum** belonging to that company. You can add an optional title. If you change the company, choose the forum again before scheduling.

If a destination is missing, check that the connected Whop account can access it. See the [channels overview](/docs/channels) for general connection and scheduling guidance.
