Dribbble is a showcase for designers. Connecting it lets PostDelegate publish scheduled shots to your profile.

## What you need

- A Dribbble account. Dribbble only lets accounts with upload rights publish shots publicly, so check that yours can upload shots before you schedule.

## Connect

1. On the calendar page choose **Add Channel**, then **Dribbble**.
2. Sign in with Dribbble and approve the permissions. PostDelegate asks to read your public profile and to upload shots.
3. You come back to PostDelegate and the channel appears on your calendar.

## What gets published

- Each post becomes a shot with exactly one image. Video and MP4 files are not supported.
- The post text becomes the shot description.
- Images are cropped from the centre to 800x600, or to 400x300 for smaller images. The minimum size is 400x300.
- GIFs are not cropped, so they must already be exactly 400x300 or 800x600.

## Post settings

| Setting | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Title | Required. The title of the shot. |

## Troubleshooting

- **The shot is rejected.** Dribbble's reason appears on the calendar. Check that your account has upload rights.
- **The channel asks to reconnect.** Dribbble stopped accepting the saved sign-in. Use **Reconnect channel** and sign in with Dribbble again. See [Reconnecting a channel](/docs/channels#reconnecting-a-channel).
