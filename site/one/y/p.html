What is planned

  • Plan business content alongside the rest of your posts.
  • Review the destination location when connecting and composing.
  • Keep updates and their media together in the composer.

Before connecting

  • A Google login does not by itself connect a Business Profile.

Frequently asked questions

Can I schedule Google Business Profile posts with PostDelegate today?
Not yet. Google Business Profile cannot be connected on PostDelegate today. It opens once Google approves Business Profile access, and a network that is not marked available in the platform directory does not work.
When will Google Business Profile be available?
We do not give dates, because platform review timelines are outside our control. When a connection opens it will appear under Add Channel in the app and get its own guide in the documentation. Contact support to tell us you need it.
What can I do on PostDelegate while I wait?
Start the 7-day free trial, with no credit card, and connect any of the 16 networks open today, such as YouTube, Bluesky, WordPress and Slack. The calendar, composer and media library work the same way for every channel.
Can Claude or ChatGPT post to Google Business Profile?
Not yet. An assistant connected over MCP can only work with channels that are connected in PostDelegate, so it cannot publish to Google Business Profile until that connection exists. You can still use Claude or ChatGPT to draft the text, then bring it into PostDelegate. See the MCP guide.
